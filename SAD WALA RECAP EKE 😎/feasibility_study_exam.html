What is a Feasibility Study?

  • Evaluates if a proposed system is worth developing
  • Checks technical possibility
  • Assesses operational acceptability
  • Confirms economic viability
  • Conducted before system design
  • Helps make go / no-go decisions
  • Reduces risk of system failure
🎯

Objectives of a Feasibility Study

  • Assess practicality of the proposed system
  • Identify potential risks and constraints
  • Estimate costs, benefits, and timelines
  • Ensure alignment with organizational goals
  • Avoid investing resources in infeasible projects
📋

6 Types of Feasibility

  • Technical feasibility
  • Operational feasibility
  • Economic feasibility
  • Schedule feasibility
  • Legal feasibility
  • Political / Cultural feasibility
💡

Importance of Feasibility Study

  • Prevents project failure
  • Saves organizational resources
  • Supports strategic decision making
  • Improves system success rate
  • Part of the System Analysis phase
  • Reduces investment risk
💰

Economic Feasibility – Costs

  • Development costs
  • Hardware and software costs
  • Training costs
  • Maintenance and operational costs
  • Hidden and indirect costs
📈

Economic Feasibility – Benefits

  • Tangible: Reduced labor cost
  • Tangible: Increased productivity
  • Intangible: Improved customer satisfaction
  • Intangible: Better decision making
  • AKA: Cost-benefit analysis
